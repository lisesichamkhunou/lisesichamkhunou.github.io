About School

The L.I. Standard English School, Icham Khunou was established in 1992 in the name of School Founder Shri L. Ibomcha Meitei, S/o, (L) L. Iboton Meitei of Icham Khunou, with the aim and object of creating a model School with English as the medium of instruction, to impart quality education to sprouting young minds, which had been the long-left need in the area for many years and also with the ambition of philanthropy. From a humble beginning, the school has now become a school of repute in the area with its commendable achievements in the last few years.

Our Motto

“Learn, Strive and Strengthen physical and mental alertness to achieve the goal.”

Our Salient Features

  • English as the medium of Instruction.
  • Co-Education setup.
  • Recognised by the Government of Manipur.
  • Special attention dedicated to weak students.
  • Waiver of tuition fees for the third or fourth child of the same parent.
  • Tuition/Coaching facilities available at nominal fees.
  • Boarding Facilities: (a) For Pre-Matriculation (b) From Class II-X, with separate arrangements for Boys and Girls.
  • Audio Visual, Computer Facilities, and Smart Class Integration.
